🔊 SOUND GUIDE • WHAT WORKS BEST

Make sure your audio is clearly audible! The procedural DSP engine extracts pitch, transients, and rhythm from your sound.
Great sounds: Street traffic, loud room chatter, speaking words aloud, singing, clapping, keys, banging pots.
⚠️ Avoid faint static, silent rooms, faint fan hums, or quiet mouse clicks — if the sound is too low, the music won't have enough signal to build from!
